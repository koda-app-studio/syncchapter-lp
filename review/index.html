SyncChapter — Sample Files

Files for trying SyncChapter during App Review. Tap each file and save it to the Files app (On My iPhone / On My iPad), then import it in the app.

  • sample.m4aAudio, 3 min 22 s, about 4 MB
  • sample.lrcTimed text for the lyrics view

Steps

  1. In SyncChapter, tap “ファイルを読み込む” (Import Files), or tap + and then “ファイルから” (From Files). Choose sample.m4a.
  2. Play it and open the full player. Tap “同期編集” (Edit Sync), tap “打刻” (Stamp) while it plays, then “保存” (Save).
  3. For lyrics, tap “歌詞” (Lyrics), the clipboard icon, then “ファイル” (File), choose sample.lrc, and tap “読み込む” (Import).
